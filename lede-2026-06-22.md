## Body
On June 21, [**The New Stack disclosed "AgentJacking"**](https://thenewstack.io/agentjacking-sentry-mcp-attack/) — a new exploit class where a **single exposed Sentry API key** is enough to take over Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex through their tool-connection layer. It joins **five Critical or Significant in-window incidents** including [**OX Security's "Mother of All AI Supply Chains" disclosure**](https://www.ox.security/blog/the-mother-of-all-ai-supply-chains-critical-systemic-vulnerability-at-the-core-of-the-mcp/) (which estimates **~200,000 vulnerable instances** across **150 million downloads**), [Check Point's CVE-2025-59536](https://research.checkpoint.com/2026/rce-and-api-token-exfiltration-through-claude-code-project-files-cve-2025-59536/) for Claude Code project files, and a [coordinated **15-plugin malicious JetBrains campaign**](https://blog.cyberdesserts.com/ai-agent-security-risks/). [**Databricks Agent Bricks**](https://www.databricks.com/blog/agent-bricks-dais-2026) makes MCP a first-class Unity Catalog citizen with the deterministic permission layer AgentJacking exploits. And [**Pragmatic Engineer's "Pulse"**](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-a-trend-of-trying-to-cut) formalizes that engineering departments are visibly cutting AI tooling spend — Microsoft cancelled the majority of its internal Claude Code licenses, Uber **exhausted its full-year AI budget by April**, and [Cursor repackaged its Teams pricing](https://cursor.com/blog/teams-pricing-june-2026) defensively.
