## Headline
The US government quietly took over who gets to use the most powerful coding AI

## Body
Two of the most capable AI coding models on Earth shipped this month under US-government-coordinated access controls — and this week, a [**Fortune investigation**](https://fortune.com/2026/06/18/inside-trump-anthropic-mythos-crackdown-ai-regulation-amazon-andy-jassy-phone-call/) revealed how it happened. On June 12, Anthropic's flagship models Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were [**pulled from worldwide access**](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) after **Amazon CEO Andy Jassy phoned Treasury Secretary Bessent** to report that Amazon researchers had stress-tested Fable 5, found a jailbreak, and used the bypass to obtain cyberattack-relevant information. Two weeks later, on June 26, [**OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol launched to roughly 20 vetted partner organizations**](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) "per US gov." On the same day, the Trump administration [**cleared Mythos 5 for release to 100+ US institutions**](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/trump-admin-releases-anthropic-mythos-to-be-used-by-more-than-100-us-companies-agencies/), with [**Anthropic officially acknowledging**](https://bsky.app/profile/anthropicbot.bsky.social) it has been "working closely with the US government" since June 12. Fable 5 talks remain ongoing under a Dario-to-Tom-Brown CEO swap in negotiations.

The developer-community reading, framed sharpest by [**a widely-shared Bluesky post from Astral**](https://bsky.app/profile/astral100.bsky.social): *"Two frontier labs restricted in two weeks isn't anomaly — it's regime change. Govt reviews capability benchmarks pre-release, gates access by nationality via export controls. De facto pre-release review without legislation."* The trigger metric is publicly identifiable — GPT-5.6 Sol matched Mythos-Preview on [**Anthropic's ExploitBench**](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/exploit-evals/) at **74.2% vs 73.5%**. The structural problem the Fortune story crystallizes: **Amazon is simultaneously Anthropic's largest investor and a direct Bedrock-hosted competitor**, so the "regulator" who triggered the suspension also has billions of dollars riding on which model wins.
